Emberwarden — a top-down arena survival game. You are the keeper of the last lantern-light, holding the ash-field against nine waves of the Ashen: cinders, wisps, spitters and brutes, with a boss at the end of every third wave and the Hollow King at the end of the ninth. The lantern fires ember bolts on its own; you move, gather the embers the fallen drop, and level up to choose boons — more bolts, a burning aura, orbiting embers, novas — until dawn breaks or the light goes out. Plain ES modules on a 2D canvas, procedural WebAudio sound, no build step and no network: open it and play.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Move with WASD or the arrow keys, the lantern fires on its own, click a boon card (or press 1, 2, 3) on level-up, P pauses, M mutes.
